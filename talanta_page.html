Getting there

Address
Raila Odinga International Stadium, Talanta Sports City, Ngong Rd, Nairobi. Get directions
Your gate and entrance are printed on your ticket.
Flying in
Fly to Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (NBO), then drive about 25 km to the stadium.

Questions

How do I get my tickets?

By SMS and e-mail straight after you pay. They are also under My tickets: log in with the phone or e-mail you bought with.

Can I sit next to my friends?

Yes. Choose how many tickets, then "Find my seats": up to 4 seats side by side. Bigger groups get blocks close together, for example 4 + 2.

What does 'No Re-entry' mean?

The No Re-entry policy for the inaugural match means that once you enter the stadium and your ticket is scanned, you will not be allowed to leave and return using the same ticket. Please ensure you have everything you need before entering the stadium.

How can I pay?

M-Pesa or card. You see the full total, with the processing fee, before you pay.
